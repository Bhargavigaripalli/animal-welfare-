Rapid Rescue Response

24/7 Animal Distress Triage Protocol

If you have spotted an injured, abandoned, or abused animal, here is how our rapid emergency dispatch system prioritizes and deploys veterinary rescue ambulances.

Code Red: Immediate (15 Min)

Life-Threatening Trauma

Hit-by-car victims with arterial bleeding, collapsed animals, respiratory distress, heatstroke, or acute poisoning.

  • • Fully equipped trauma ambulance dispatched
  • • In-transit digital vitals & oxygen telemetry
  • • Surgical suite prepped upon hospital arrival
Call Emergency Hotline
Code Orange: Urgent (45 Min)

Neonatal & Severe Neglect

Abandoned newborn litters without maternal heat, animals trapped in storm drains, severe mange, or non-ambulatory injuries.

  • • Mobile incubator & thermal transport deployed
  • • Colostrum syringe feeding kit prepared
  • • Direct transfer to specialized Neonatal Nursery
Code Yellow: Same-Day

Cruelty & Community Strays

Ongoing chain tethering, hoarding situations, limping community strays, or abandoned domestic pets reported in vacant lots.

  • • Humane animal welfare investigation officer
  • • Food replenishment and safe crate trapping
  • • Full intake examination and zero-kill sheltering

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tax deductibility, monthly management, and financial governance.

Is my gift 100% tax-deductible under federal law?
Yes. PawsHaven is an authorized 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charitable organization (EIN: 84-2947190). Immediately upon donation completion, an official IRS-compliant tax receipt is generated and emailed to you for your tax records.
How do I cancel, pause, or adjust my recurring Guardian gift?
You maintain 100% control over your monthly pledge at all times. Every receipt includes a self-serve donor management link allowing you to update payment details, alter contribution tiers, pause, or cancel with a single click.
Can I dedicate my contribution in memory or in honor of a pet?
Yes! When submitting your donation, select the memorial dedication checkbox to specify the animal or loved one being honored. We will generate a commemorative digital certificate and list their name on our sanctuary Memorial Roll.
How much of my dollar goes directly to the animals vs administration?
According to our 2025 audited financial statements, exactly 99.2 cents of every dollar donated directly funds veterinary surgery, medical supplies, and sanctuary food. Only 0.8 cents is spent on payment processing and audit administration.

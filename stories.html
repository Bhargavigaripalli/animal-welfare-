Barnaby: From The Brink of Despair to Pediatric Therapy Star

When our 24/7 Mobile Vet unit arrived on Route 9, Barnaby was curled up in freezing mud, unable to stand on his shattered left hind leg. Suffering from acute hypothermia, sarcoptic mange, and intestinal parasites, the prognosis was guarded.

Thanks to an emergency appeal that raised $4,200 within 48 hours, Dr. Sarah Mitchell performed a complex three-hour femoral head surgery. Four months of aquatic hydrotherapy and medicated baths healed his coat into a lustrous golden sheen. Last week, Barnaby passed his evaluation to visit children in hospital oncology wards!

"Barnaby brings so much joy into our home. When we look into his warm amber eyes, we see the pure power of human kindness." — Sophia Ross, Adopter

Luna: The 200-Gram Fighter Who Wouldn't Give Up

Municipal workers clearing snow heard faint, desperate mewing inside a concrete drainage culvert. Luna was barely three weeks old, eyes crusted shut with severe herpesviral conjunctivitis, weighing less than an apple.

Our neonatal intensive care team placed her in a temperature-regulated oxygen incubator with syringe feedings every 2 hours. Today, Luna is a playful, acrobatic calico who enjoys sunny windowsills and snuggling on her human's chest.

Leo: From Entangled Roadside Ghost to Sanctuary Champion

Found trapped in rusted construction wire with severe dehydration and leg lacerations, Leo's will to survive inspired our entire surgical squad.

Day 1: Rescue ICU
Month 4: Radiant & Healthy
112 Days of Restorative Medicine

"He Looked Up With Trust Even While in Acute Pain"

When our 24/7 Mobile Vet Squad arrived on Highway 9, Leo was pinned beneath heavy barbed fencing. His vital signs were critical. Over the next four months, Dr. Marcus and our surgical nursing staff performed three reconstructive surgeries, daily antimicrobial honey dressings, and canine physical therapy.

  • 3 successful soft-tissue microsurgeries with zero nerve loss
  • Full weight recovery from 19 lbs to a muscular 42 lbs
  • Now living happily on a 15-acre organic farm with his adoptive family
